Leader Affiliative Humor on Employee AI Adoption Behavior
Ongoing Thesis
Theoretical Framework: Transactional Stress-and-Coping Theory (Lazarus & Folkman)

Investigating whether leader affiliative humor acts as an interpersonal coping resource to alleviate employee algorithmic anxiety, fostering greater AI adoption under varying levels of implementation intensity. Features a multi-organization empirical design targeting 300–350 respondents across banking, telecom, and healthcare sectors using Hayes' PROCESS Model 4 and custom non-linear moderated-mediation polynomial specifications.
